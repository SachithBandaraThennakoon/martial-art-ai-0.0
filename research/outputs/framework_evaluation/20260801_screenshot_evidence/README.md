# Screenshot evidence bundle — 2026-08-01

This bundle preserves 78 screenshots supplied by the researcher from a jab-only,
single-participant expert self-evaluation using the laptop camera. Files were
copied without alteration. SHA-256 hashes are in `artifact_manifest.csv`.

## Privacy and use

The images show the researcher, account name, local routes, and session details.
They are `restricted_identifiable`. Do not commit or publish uncropped images
without review and permission. Derived figures should obscure names and identity.

## Evidence classification

These are functional and observational evidence that interfaces, outputs, state
transitions, and diagnostics were displayed. Alone, they cannot establish model
accuracy, latency, causal LLM reasoning, baseline superiority, generalization,
clinical safety, or human-equivalent cognition. Current feedback is deterministic
rule/template output, not a verified operational LLM.

## Folder interpretation

- `train-hard mode/`: 19-frame sequence covering personalized greeting, waiting,
  voice response, prioritized angle correction, step transition, guard-related
  correction, completion choice, train-again voice choice, and restart.
- `train -easy mode/`: easier-guidance sequence plus hand-tracking test.
- `practice mode/`: setup/live output and full-session timeline analysis with
  selectable moments/clusters, corrected skeleton, and session history.
- `analysis mode/`: post-session analysis views.
- `admin/`: Level 1 yellow observation, Level 2 blue ACP-STGAT forecast, combined
  overlay, live diagnostics, advanced Level 2 panels, L1–L4 data, Skeleton Lab.
- `dashboard/`: overview, performance, techniques, activity, sessions, and global
  date/mode/category/technique/difficulty/status/accuracy/focus filters.
- `pages/`: public/authenticated pages, plans/payment, technique selection, and
  Train/Practice/Analysis Studio-mode selection.
